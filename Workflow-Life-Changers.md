## Candidates (not yet carded)

### 1. Capture MIDI  ⭐ (high impact now)
Noodle on the keys **without** recording, then hit **Capture** — Ableton retroactively grabs what you
just played as an in-time clip. Never lose a happy accident again. Pairs with the "arm the track" habit.
*Why life-changer:* removes the pressure of hitting record; every good idea is recoverable.

### 2. Build & save your own Racks + Macros, and a device-preset library  ⭐ (high impact now)
The unlock isn't using a rack once — it's **saving your go-to chains** (bass chain, a "growl design"
rack, your master chain) and your **own default device presets** (your EQ8 starting point, your comp
settings) so you drop them in instantly. Right-click a device → **Save as default preset**; drag a rack
to the browser to save it; one Macro knob can control many params at once.
*Why life-changer:* you stop rebuilding the same chains every project — compounding time-save.

### 3. Browser Collections (color tags) + Hot-Swap (Q)
Tag your favorite sounds/devices/samples into **colored Collections** so your ~20 go-to things are one
click away instead of buried in thousands. **Hot-Swap (Q)** auditions samples/presets *in place* while
a clip plays.
*Why life-changer:* kills "scrolling the browser forever"; keeps you in creative flow.

### 4. Freeze & Flatten / commit to audio
**Freeze** a CPU-heavy track (stacked Serum growls), **Flatten** to turn it into audio, or bounce-in-place.
Frees CPU *and* commits the sound so you stop endlessly tweaking. The Ableton-wide version of the
**resampling** move already used in Serum.
*Why life-changer:* keeps big projects playable and forces decisions (anti-tweak-forever).

### 5. Scale / Key mode (Live 12)  ⭐ (high impact now — good for a non-theory producer)
Set the song key, turn on **Scale mode**, and the piano roll **folds to / highlights in-key notes** —
everything you draw stays in key.
*Why life-changer:* huge confidence + speed for melodies and basslines without deep theory.

### 6. Consolidate (Cmd+J) + Duplicate (Cmd+D) for arranging
Loop a section → **Cmd+J** to consolidate → **Cmd+D** to duplicate and extend. Build an arrangement in
minutes instead of dragging clips around.
*Why life-changer:* the backbone of fast arranging.

### 7. Reference-track A/B workflow
Keep a pro track on a **muted channel** you can instantly un-mute to compare loudness / tone / width.
(The *mastering* reference concept is already carded; the **always-loaded, one-key-away workflow** is the
life-changer part.)
*Why life-changer:* grounds every mix decision against a real target.
