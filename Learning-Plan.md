## The decided path (front-load creating)
1. **Dubstep masterclass (~45 min)** — do this FIRST. Get the genre framework/skeleton (tempo/half-time, intro→build→drop→breakdown→2nd build→2nd drop→outro, sub+growl as the hook, risers/impacts, drop = centerpiece). *Method: watch all the way through once for the framework, THEN rewatch sections while building.*
2. **Start a daily rep NOW** (even 20 min) — a bassline, drum groove, growl, or 8-bar loop. Use the Reps tab. This is the real unlock; do it in parallel, not after.
3. **Build ONE full track from Splice samples** — structure/flow/mixing practice with the sound-design burden removed. Training wheels.
4. **First original creative track** — the goal. Momentum by now, not just theory.
