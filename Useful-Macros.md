# Useful Macros — recipes to recreate in Ableton

**Standing idea:** collect single-knob **Macro** setups worth reusing (from Guido, or discovered in exercises). Each lists what the knob does + the parameter mappings (`param · min → max`) so Charlie can rebuild them in his own Sets. Mirrored in the app's **Macros** tab.

## Open-up (bright + wide) — via Guido
One knob morphs the sound from **dark + narrow/mono → bright + wide**. Turn **up** to "open" (brighter, wider, powerful); turn **down** to "close" (darker, more mono/mid, focused). Great for builds, breakdowns, and transitions.

Mappings (all on **Macro 1**):
- **EQ Eight · Band 8 Frequency:** 800 Hz → 22 kHz (opens the top end / brightens)
- **Roar · Blend (Mid/Side):** 60/40 (more Mid = mono/centered) → 40/60 (more Side = wide)

_Note: the Roar "Blend" here is the **Mid/Side** balance (Roar in Mid Side routing), not a sidechain._

## 1 Knob Open (advanced) — via Guido
The Open-up macro **elevated**: one knob morphs the whole sound from **CLOSED** (dark, narrow/mono, tight, single-layer) → **OPEN** (bright, wide, evolving, layered, powerful). Same open/close philosophy, more reach — it now also shapes the envelope, adds glide, and fades in Meld's second engine for thickness + stereo. Great for driving a whole section's energy.

Mappings (all on **Macro "1 Knob Open"**):
- **Meld · A Amp Env Attack:** 11 → 48 ms (softer onset as it opens)
- **Meld · A Amp Env Decay:** 200 → 500 ms (more body / sustain)
- **Meld · A Glide Time:** 29 → 48 ms (a touch more glide)
- **Meld · B Volume:** −50 → −12 dB (fades the 2nd engine in — thickness + stereo)
- **Roar · Blend (Mid/Side):** 60/40 → 40/60 (wider)
- **EQ Eight · Band 8 Frequency:** 700 Hz → 22 kHz (brighter)
